P2 — FOREST MOON (original e097c16 → 3db0da7)

New circuit built with the same Track/layout() API (layout(points, rest) returns {ctrl, at, loops, gaps, tunnels, caves, pads, gates, flips, towers:false, meteor:false, canopies, covers, theme, …}).

ForestWorld + atmo.js: ONE atmosphere model for sky and everything seen through it: two extinction layers with height falloff — air (scale height 8 km, much thicker in blue) and jungle haze (grey, scale height 350 m, thick at canopy) — closed-form integrated along the ray; in-scatter = sun through air phase + haze's strong forward lobe (glare) + sky light, × (1 − transmittance). Two cloud decks (one in the abyss under the circuit with gaps showing canopy; one high). Dome evaluates to infinity; every lit material gets it through the fog chunk. Below the horizon the jungle, not endless haze (no grey band, no grey light from below on ships).
abyss.js: jungle canopy to horizon ~1.3 km down: ONE mesh on a polar grid round the circuit (fine under it, coarse far); crowns, colours, clearings, rivers drawn per pixel from world pos (cellular noise: each cell a crown lit as a dome); rivers as sinuous distance fields.
islands.js: floating islands, each ONE closed surface generated in rows from the top middle down to the lowest tip (grassy top with gentle hills, rounded rim, layered-rock cliffs, underside in lobes). Rock = GPU tile hex-tiled from 3 axes at 2 scales; strata, water streaks, moss over rim and down streaks, grass on top from world position.
flora.js: broadleaf trees, emergent giants with buttress roots, palms, fern bushes, vines. Leaf atlas painted once on canvas (each shape twice: colour over leaf-green and white coverage, so mips never bleed black); trees built in code (tubes for wood, leaf cards in clusters whose normals point out of the cluster so a crown shades as one soft mass); leaves sway/flutter and glow where sun shines through; everything instanced per sector of the island field; far sectors swap to coarser trees.
water.js: waterfalls: a river leaves a lip at some speed and falls along p = lip + out*v*t − g t²/2; the mesh is laid out in time since the lip (rows finer near it) so features ride down accelerating; solid + foaming at lip, ropes lower, then spray plumes lit by sun with a rainbow at 42° when the sun is at your back. The Cascade river arches over the track.
creatures.js: sky rays (14–26 m, pods on slow loops, wing wave from body to tips), bird flocks (GPU flight from flock loop + own offset + time), sky whales (~200 m, distant). Paths avoid island bounds and track.
Lens water pass (LensWaterPass): a draining film, uneven drops that refract, blown outward by the air; spray off ships through a waterfall; light-shaft pass (ShaftPass) only from broad light (a glint/spark/engine core must not be drawn out into lines).
Liana bridges, vines on the deck (overgrowth.js: strands hang straight down from edges clear of the deck at any bank, none in loop/rolls/over jumps; ivy up gantry pillars; none reaches into ships' way: gantries stop ~8 m over the deck).
skeleton.js (commit 3db0da7): a colossal skull at circuit height; track in through the jaws (between fangs, under palate ribs), out of the back of the skull over a cut; neck plunging 1.2 km to a rib cage in the jungle; lianas wound down the neck, trees on the cranium, vine curtains from the jaws. Skull built like a reptile's: bony bars round nostril, antorbital window, eye socket, temporal openings, tooth rows, crest of horns. Lap +2.7 km.
